About us

Live in a Better Aura

Mr. Livora helps housing societies run with less paperwork, clearer finances and calmer communication — so neighbours can spend more time being a community.

Our story

Society life deserves better tools

Most societies still run on paper registers, spreadsheets and busy group chats. Dues are hard to follow, receipts go missing and important notices get buried.

We built Mr. Livora so committees can manage properties, residents, maintenance and payments in one place, and every resident can see what matters to their home.

Our mission

Make every society transparent, private and simple to run — with the committee in charge and residents always informed.
